About Me

I hold a PhD in Mathematics from the Université de Fianarantsoa, Madagascar, completed in October 2026. My research focuses on algebraic combinatorics, particularly combinatorial species, symmetric functions, and representation theory.

My doctoral thesis, Categorification of the Cycle Index Series, develops a categorical framework for studying cycle index series associated with combinatorial structures and their relationships with Schur positivity, plethysm, and Kronecker products.

My doctoral research was conducted under the supervision of François Bergeron (Université du Québec à Montréal, Canada) and Benjamin Randrianarivina (Université de Fianarantsoa).

My doctoral studies were supported by the International Mathematical Union through the GRAID programme .

Research

My research lies at the intersection of algebraic combinatorics, combinatorial species, symmetric functions, and representation theory. I am particularly interested in the algebraic structures arising from cycle index series and their connections with the Kronecker product.

Combinatorial Species

Cycle index series, combinatorial constructions, categorification, and morphisms between species-theoretic frameworks.

Symmetric Functions

Schur positivity, plethysm, molecular bases, and structural properties of symmetric functions.

Representation Theory

Symmetric groups, character theory, Kronecker products, and connections with algebraic combinatorics.

Computational Combinatorics

Symbolic computation, experimental mathematics, and algorithmic investigations using SageMath.
